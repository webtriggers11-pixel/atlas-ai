# MediBook Patient Portal — Requirements

## Appointments
The portal must send an appointment reminder by SMS 24 hours before the visit.
Patients must be able to cancel an appointment up to 2 hours before its start time.
Doctors' availability must be synchronised from the clinic scheduling system every 5 minutes.

## Accounts and Security
Users must be logged out automatically after 20 minutes of inactivity.
The portal must support two-factor authentication using an authenticator app.
All access to medical records must be recorded in an audit trail that is retained for 6 years.
The system must comply with HIPAA.
Uploaded documents must be scanned for malware before they are stored.

## Results and Accessibility
Lab results must be visible to patients within 48 hours of being finalised.
The portal must be usable on screens as narrow as 320 pixels.
